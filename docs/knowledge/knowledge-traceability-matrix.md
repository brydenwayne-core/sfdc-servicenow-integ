# Knowledge Traceability Matrix

## Purpose
Prevent knowledge drift by mapping concise Salesforce/ServiceNow knowledge articles to their long-form repository source documents.

## Update Policy
Update a knowledge article whenever one of its source-of-truth documents changes in a way that affects steps, ownership, status interpretation, field mappings, escalation criteria, or operational sequencing.
